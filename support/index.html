A street that felt wrong

Tap the street, then Felt worse or Felt better under the direction you rode. Flags stay on your phone; to send them, open Settings → Ride flags → Export flags and attach the file to an email. The most useful reports name the street, the direction and what made it feel that way: "a double-parked truck every block" can be checked against the data in a way a score can't.

Common questions

Is a calm street a safe street?

No. A score estimates how calm a street is likely to feel, from public data: speed limits, lanes, traffic, trucks, buses, bike lanes, crashes and pavement. It can't see today's traffic, weather or construction the city hasn't published. Obey signs and traffic laws, and ride with care.

Why is a street grey, or never in a route?

Grey streets are ones your moped class can't use, by our reading of the rules: highways, the bridges mopeds are barred from, car-free park drives, paths, and the wrong way down one-way streets. It's a reading, not legal advice; the signs on the street take precedence.

Why did a bus lane stop counting?

Part-time bus lanes follow their posted hours. Outside them, a bus lane is the right-hand lane you ride in, so the street scores as it does then. Routes always assume they're in force.

Does my location leave my phone?

No. Routes are worked out on your phone, and your location is used only there. See the privacy policy.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The street data is in the app; it checks for newer data when it can, and keeps what it has when it can't. Address search needs a connection.
