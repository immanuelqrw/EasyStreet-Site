Easy Street — Privacy Policy

Last updated 1 November 2026.

Easy Street is a map of how calm New York City streets are likely to feel on a moped, with route finding. It is built to work without knowing who you are.

What we collect: nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. It doesn't send information about you or your use of the app to us or to anyone else.

Your location. If you allow it, the app uses your location on your phone to start a route from where you are and to show you on the map. It isn't stored by the app or sent anywhere. You can turn location access off at any time in iOS Settings.

Address search. When you search for an address or place, the search text goes to Apple's map search service, which is covered by Apple's privacy policy. Easy Street doesn't keep your searches.

Routes. Routes are worked out on your phone from street data bundled with the app; your start and destination aren't sent anywhere.

Ride flags. Streets you flag as feeling better or worse than their score are saved only on your phone. They leave it only if you export them yourself (for example by AirDrop or email). You can delete them in Settings → Ride flags.

Street data updates. The app downloads updated street data from the Easy Street website, which is hosted by GitHub Pages. The download asks only for the data files and carries nothing about you; like any web request, it reaches GitHub's servers, whose own policy applies.

Apple Watch. If you use the watch app, the phone sends it your current directions and recent places directly, over Apple's own connection between your phone and your watch. They don't go to us or anyone else.

Links. Street View and data-source links open in your browser, where those sites' own policies apply.

Children. The app collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.

Contact. Questions about this policy or the app: easystreet@patacognition.tech.
